How I got here

The quiet

Before the Midnight Nursery, there was one quiet hill and one very small skull baby who hated silence. Pip couldn’t sleep. Neither could the little ghosts down the lane.

The first lullaby

In an attic, Pip found a dusty music box with one key and five notes. Pip wound it up. Every little ghost on the street yawned at the same time.

The job

The Midnight Nursery hired Pip on the spot. Now Pip builds dreams after dark: lullabies, nightlights, and bedtime tales. Still a little clumsy. Still giggling.
